Methods

How the claims were tested

Every comparison on this page has a test behind it. A claim counts as significant when its p-value stays under 0.05 after a Holm correction across all the claims at once, which guards against finding something by chance when testing many things. Ranges are 95% intervals, from bootstraps with a fixed seed or from the model. A claim that fails is reworded on the page, not removed.

Claim Test Effect 95% range p Holm p Result

Night means a yard starting between 00:00 and 05:59 local time. The night-odds model compares stopping in night and day hours within the same 6-yard stage of the race and leaves out every 24th yard, where milestone stops pile up. Two notes on the team tests: both runners' yards are part of the team total they're compared with, and the nations aren't a random sample, so those tests describe these championships rather than prove a general rule.

Terms

Yard
One 4.167-mile loop. A new one starts every hour, so 24 yards is 100 miles in a day.
National winner
The last runner standing in a nation, who must then finish one more yard alone. If the last runners stop on the same yard, there is no winner.
Assist
The runner who stopped last before the winner.
Team score
Total yards completed by a nation's 15 runners.
