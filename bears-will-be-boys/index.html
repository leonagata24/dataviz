How the books were read

  • The Pudding picked highly rated English-language picture books on Goodreads with at least one animal that talks or acts like a person, about 30 per decade since 1950, plus older classics.
  • A character counts as he/him or she/her only when the text says so: pronouns, titles like Mr. or Mother, or a gendered name. Illustrations and clothing were not used.
  • One row in the data is one character. A book can have many, so the 846 characters come from 284 books.

Notes on the method

  • Counts are of characters, not books, unless a chart says otherwise. The Goodreads link identifies a book; two editions of The Three Billy Goats Gruff are kept apart because they are different books.
  • Animal groups follow the dataset’s animal_group column, which folds names together (bunny into rabbit, kitten into cat). The chip on each book shows the name used in the text.
  • Ratings and rating counts are Goodreads figures at the time The Pudding collected them. They will have moved since. The scatter uses a log scale for the number of ratings.
  • Covers load from Goodreads. If a cover does not load, the page shows the title instead.
