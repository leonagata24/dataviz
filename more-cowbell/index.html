A fan-built list of songs that feature a cowbell. We matched each one to its release year on MusicBrainz, then asked three questions: who uses the cowbell most, when it peaked, and where it turns up when you least expect it.

Not just for rock bands

The list is mostly guitar rock, but cowbell also turns up in comedy records, a musical and even a polka. A few of the stranger corners.

Illustration made for this page.

The list owes its fame to one sketch. In April 2000, Saturday Night Live imagined the recording of Blue Öyster Cult's “Don't Fear the Reaper”, with Will Ferrell on cowbell and Christopher Walken as the producer who kept asking for more.
