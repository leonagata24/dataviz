    Method and notes

    • Source. The MakeoverMonday 2026 Week 25 dataset, 1,317 NUFORC reports, each with images attached. The Kaggle page it originally came from has been removed.
    • Map. Albers USA projection, with Alaska and Hawaii moved into insets. Reports that share coordinates (usually a city centre) are drawn as one dot sized by count.
    • Population. 2020 Census resident population for the 50 states and DC. The rate counts reports in this sample, not all UFO reports.
    • Shape groups. Points of light: Light, Orb, Fireball, Star. Round: Circle, Sphere, Disk, Oval, Egg. Angular: Triangle, Chevron, Diamond, Rectangle, Cross, Cone, Teardrop. Long and thin: Cigar, Cylinder. Formation, Changing and Other/Unknown stay as reported.
    • Duration. Free text such as “5-10 min”, “Two Minutes” or “from 3:16 am to 5:30 am” was parsed to seconds, using the first number of a range. Entries such as “Found in pic” count as instant or photo only. Anything else (“Ongoing”, “Not sure”) is unclear.
    • Time of day. The hour of the reported time. About one report in four gives a time on the hour or half hour, so these are approximate.
    • Report links. Each report links to the NUFORC page given in the dataset. NUFORC has since rebuilt its site, so some links may no longer open.
